Incident lifecycle

From first report to cleared road

Modelled on the traffic incident management timeline: detection, verification, response and clearance — with a freshness clock so unconfirmed reports never linger.

Reported

A citizen drops a pin, picks a type and describes what they see.

Verified

Reputation-weighted "still active" confirmations cross the threshold.

Assigned

An officer takes ownership or the control room dispatches one.

Resolved

Outcome recorded; reputations of reporters and confirmers settle.

Stale  Not re-confirmed within the freshness window? The report fades out until someone confirms it again.

Citizens

The eyes on the road.

  • Report with location & photo
  • Confirm “still active” / “cleared”
  • Subscribe to zones & routes

Field Authorities

On-ground officers who own the case.

  • Take ownership & resolve
  • Audited blockages with expected end
  • Re-confirm or clear overdue closures

Control room

The city-wide desk view.

  • Live status counts & map
  • Dispatch verified incidents
  • Full audit trail & user admin
